About

Zulaykha is a full-stack and AI developer at BDO IT Consulting in Mauritius, building web platforms and AI-powered tools — architecting systems, writing production code across .NET, React, Python, Java and C++, wiring LLM agents into real workflows, and hardening what she ships with her own DevOps and security background. Her BSc in Electronics with Computer Science from the University of Mauritius shows up in how she treats software: as a system to be designed and built end-to-end, not assembled from parts.

Early on, she worked on the electronics and software team behind MauriVent, a citizen-built mechanical ventilator prototype developed in Mauritius during the COVID-19 pandemic — a 25-person volunteer effort that turned a manual bag-valve mask into an automated device with adjustable breathing parameters, built for roughly Rs 15,000. That instinct for building working systems under real constraints — and for building them for her community — carries straight through to what she builds today, from enterprise AI tooling to apps like HalaalMoris and a poverty-relief platform for the Muslim community.
